Know the limits

  • Educational decision support, not investment advice. Sim Hedge is not a licensed broker or insurance agent. LRP is bought through an approved livestock insurance agent.
  • Basis risk. LRP pays on the national CME Feeder Cattle Index, not your local sale barn. If your local price falls more than the index, LRP won't cover the difference. The basis you enter is an assumption.
  • Prices and rates change daily. The numbers shown are USDA's for the date listed. What you can buy tomorrow will differ.
  • Timing. LRP pays on its end date, which may not match your sale date exactly.
  • History check simplifications. Past results use USDA's real published prices for Steers 600-1,000 lbs, scaled by USDA's price adjustment factor for other cattle types, and assume your herd size and weight. Past results don't predict future ones.
  • Premiums are billed at the end of coverage. Head limits and other policy rules apply; check with an agent.
